# Skeptic: independent construct-validity review

Scope: three fixed v1 primary texts, read before peer outputs. This is a methods audit, not a computational replication. The runtime inherits the coordinator's model; separate prompts and a sealed first pass reduce information leakage but do not establish independent model errors. All proposed tests below are my analysis, not results already obtained.

## ASAL — arXiv:2412.17799v1

Claim 1, favorable: foundation-model representations make a reusable discovery interface across unlike substrates credible (§§3–4, Figs. 3–6). The CA experiment averages 256 initializations, not one (Appendix B.2). Critical alternative: 2,048-step, 32-frame historical embedding novelty measures observer-relative finite-horizon change; it does not demonstrate indefinitely productive evolution. A wandering transient could score well without accumulating capabilities. The paper itself explicitly outsources observer choice to the representation (§3.2).

Claim 2: the CLIP/DINO/pixel comparison supports useful perceptual search, but its qualitative outcome is weaker evidence for biological agency or intelligence (§4.5, Fig. 8). Rendering, color, crop, and frame schedule can change what is rewarded without changing underlying dynamics. Single-target search also evaluates one initialization per simulation (B.1); that limitation should not be incorrectly applied to CA search.

Discriminating followup: freeze selected rules, evaluate held-out initializations and 10x/100x horizons, and counterbalance renderings. Score both withheld embeddings and preregistered state-level persistence, reproduction, and functional adaptation. A cross-renderer, cross-horizon advantage supports substantive discovery; novelty confined to the optimization observer supports a narrower visual-search interpretation.

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.17799v1 (§§3.2,4.2,4.5; Appendices A,B.1–B.2).

## Leniabreeder — arXiv:2406.04235v1

Claim 1, favorable: sustained repertoire discovery is supported beyond attractive examples: ten random-seed replications, corrected tests, and multiple diversity measures are reported (Experiments, Fig. 5). The authors expressly disclaim theoretical open-endedness (Background: Open-Ended Evolution). The strongest defensible claim is useful sustained exploration within the evaluated regime.

Claim 2, critical alternative: new archive admissions can reflect a moving descriptor rather than new functional organization. AURORA retrains its VAE; entropy depends on that model, and raw pixel variance is not invariant to cosmetic changes (Background: AURORA; Unsupervised Diversity). Low latent variance rewards stability but need not imply agency; the authors acknowledge penalizing periodic patterns (Unsupervised Statistical Measures). Aquarium initialization and soliton constraints also select which expressions of life can be discovered (Search Space; Constraints on Growth).

Discriminating followup: retain every phenotype and rescore all checkpoints with one frozen, independently trained encoder plus rotation/scale-normalized state descriptors. Compare moving/frozen descriptors under equal budgets and multiple founders. Separately measure disturbance recovery and acquired functions. Growth in invariant functional diversity would strengthen the evolutionary interpretation; only moving-archive growth would favor descriptor drift.

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04235v1 (named sections above; Figs. 2,5; Conclusion).

## From Text to Life — arXiv:2407.09502v1

Claim 1, favorable: this is a useful conceptual bridge and research agenda, not a new controlled demonstration of living LLMs (Introduction; Discussion). It distinguishes permissive autonomy from self-sustainability and explicitly notes many agent properties are hard-coded rather than emergent (Autonomy; Emergence). Critiquing it as if it claimed experimentally established life would manufacture disagreement.

Claim 2, critical alternative: complex group behavior can come from orchestration, shared pretrained knowledge, or additional sampling budget rather than locally emergent collective intelligence. The Collective Intelligence section identifies short horizons and largely in-context learning as current limitations. Its analogies suggest testable mechanisms but cannot themselves identify those mechanisms.

Discriminating followup: randomize task instances across equal-token single-agent, independent ensemble, scripted-role team, and adaptive local-interaction team; keep tools and evidence access matched. Cross communication on/off with persistent memory on/off; include unfamiliar tasks, perturbations, and repeated generations. Report held-out accuracy, error correlation, recovery, cost, and genuinely new skills. Benefits beyond the ensemble baseline, causally dependent on interaction and persisting under perturbation, would support collective mechanisms. Role labels or fluent dialogue alone would not.

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.09502v1 (Autonomy; Emergence; Collective Intelligence; Discussion).

## Review disposition

Accept these as discovery methods and a productive conceptual agenda with bounded evidence. Preserve disagreement about which diversity deserves selection: stability may suppress interesting cycles, whereas novelty may reward meaningless drift. Neither objective universally dominates. Before promoting any artifact to an open-endedness, agency, or intelligence claim, attach its observer, rendering, search budget, founders, horizon, seed unit, exclusions, and withheld validation. No RNA-seq-specific differential-expression contract is applicable here.
