# Explorer post-reveal note

The independent reviews converge on a useful distinction: diversity is simultaneously a discovery mechanism and a measurement choice. That convergence is provisional agreement among same-model runtimes, not three independent empirical validations.

What changed: the evaluator sharpened my proposed test. Its primary sampling unit must be a complete search/team run; treating archive entries, frames or claims as independent replicates would overstate evidence. I would add independent ensemble-plus-aggregation and message-shuffling controls to my initial experiment. Equal token budgets alone do not isolate a communication mechanism. No correction to the fixed paper facts in my initial review was needed.

What remains in tension: rewarding stability can remove precisely the oscillation, switching or transient exploration that produces useful new mechanisms. Conversely, rewarding novelty can retain drift with no predictive or functional value. The resolution should be a multiobjective archive that preserves both classes with explicit tags, rather than one scalar score that silently decides what life or intelligence must look like.

Practical recommendation for H — inference, not a paper result: build a small “observer stress test” around the existing coordinator. Give teams the same candidate simulations, but allocate genuinely different evidence channels: one agent sees visual trajectories; one sees state dynamics and perturbation outcomes; one sees lineage and search metadata. Each makes a sealed classification before discussion. Ask which discoveries remain distinct when the observer changes. Then let a builder run controlled transformations and an evaluator score held-out function.

A strong hackathon deliverable would show one case where apparent diversity vanishes under an irrelevant rendering transformation and one where visually similar patterns have different damage-recovery behavior. Preserve the disagreement trail. This directly demonstrates why complementary information can matter. The present literature run supplies the hypothesis and audit trail; it has not run that simulation benchmark or established emergent cognition.
