## How to interpret the scores

Specification / discriminating power / near-term tractability / scientific leverage are separate 0–4 formulation judgments. They are neither evidence levels nor a composite merit score. ±1 bands are sensitivity assumptions. Pareto labels compare only eligible ideas within the same niche. Literature novelty is unknown for all ideas.

- **Triangle:** at E=0 and gamma=2, zero flux splits the chosen input as 20% reflection and 40% per other port; opposite pi/2 fluxes send 100% to opposite labelled ports. Eigenvalues agree; allowing output swaps makes the symmetric pair equivalent.
- **Non-normal dynamics:** equal eigenvalues -1,-2, with off-diagonal 8 versus 0, give sampled peak amplitude gains 2.080092 versus 1. This does not measure signal-to-noise benefit.
- **Nonlinear basin:** both examples have derivative -1 at zero, yet initial state .9 approaches 0 versus 1. This does not demonstrate a complete memory protocol.

- **ECA:** rule 43 has 16 distinct functions and zero bijections, while identity rule 204 has one function and preserves two input bits, under our six-site/three-step protocol. Counting functions and preserving information are different objectives.

## Corrections

- **Same spectrum means same function:** False for fixed interfaces; triangle and non-normal examples supply counterexamples.
- **Same finite measured response means gauge equivalence:** Only observational equivalence under that protocol; unobserved states or new interventions can differ.
- **Laplacian entropy measures representational diversity:** Fails a six-identical-agent negative control; graph connectivity spectra and representation diversity are separate.
- **Any new schema residual is new useful science:** Residual is schema-relative; require added predictive discrimination, robustness and complexity accounting.
- **Spectra determine mode shapes:** Eigenvalues alone do not recover eigenvectors. Retain the operator, state/interface and eigenspaces, especially at degeneracy.
- **Graph cycles automatically imply nonlinear reasoning or cohomological obstruction:** Specify state spaces, maps, faces and semantics. Hodge decomposition is linear; a sheaf may have nonzero H1 and still global sections.
- **Orthogonal agents are independent agents:** Different methods and inputs improve complementarity; shared model, data and solver errors remain.

## Evidence and implementation limits

- This is an evaluated map of a finite conversation, not a generated discovery campaign or a comprehensive literature review.
- Thirty-three canonical families are a human/AI analytical granularity choice. Aliases, substrate variants and repeated assistant answers are merged; useful submechanisms may be split in future versions.
- Three complementary reviewers used separate first-pass contexts but share the parent model family and source data. Role specialization does not establish statistical independence. No human ratings were collected.
- Four 0–4 formulation axes are distinct questions, not proven statistically orthogonal factors. They are ordinal judgments with ±1 sensitivity bands, not confidence intervals, calibrated probabilities or measured scientific quality.
- Novelty potential concerns the proposed synthesis. Literature novelty remains null for every idea; primary-source checks establish selected precedents, not absence of prior art.
- The source reports a 7,400-word notebook, five demos and 39 checks. Its sandbox files were not recovered here; those reported numbers are not counted as verified. New local controls use their own explicit protocols.
- The Sakana AI Scientist installation did not complete an AI investigation in the preceding run. It did not produce these evaluations.
- No physical device, material-specific DFT/thermodynamic calculation, new hardware advantage, or matched-budget multiagent superiority was demonstrated.
- Diversity cell occupancy is conditional on this declared schema and corpus. Empty cells are prompts for inquiry, not proof of undiscovered science.
- Review files preserve first-pass suggestions. Parent synthesis corrects overclaims and reconciles taxonomic choices; reviewer descriptions are not empirical outcomes.
